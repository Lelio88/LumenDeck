How does it work?

One press starts the scenario, a second stops it. The lamp then returns exactly to the state it was in: same colour, same brightness, on or off.

The second bulb is optional. Only Police lights uses two today, so they answer each other in red and blue. With one, it alternates both colours on the same lamp.

Alert and Sunrise stop on their own, after three flashes and five minutes respectively. The others run until you press again.

Animations are deliberately capped at two alternations per second: beyond that, flashing light can trigger a seizure in a photosensitive person.
